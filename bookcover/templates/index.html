A NOVEL OF MYSTERY AND DISCOVERY

The Silent Horizon

Some journeys begin with a map. Hers began with a secret.

When Maya inherits a weathered journal from a grandfather she never met, she finds coordinates to an island that appears on no chart. As she follows the clues across oceans and old cities, she learns that the truth is buried deeper than treasure, and that someone else is searching too.

A gripping tale of family, courage, and the question that haunts every traveller: what are you willing to leave behind to find the truth?
